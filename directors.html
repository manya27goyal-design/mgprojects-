Our Founders

The Visionaries Behind VMighty Agro

Director & Co-Founder
Director & Co-Founder

Mr. Lokesh Goyal

Mr. Goyal is a seasoned entrepreneur and Director of VMighty Agro Limited, bringing over 35 years of diverse business experience across multiple industries. A Science graduate with an entrepreneurial mindset from a young age, he began his business journey while still in school, laying the foundation for a career defined by innovation, resilience, and strategic growth.

A pioneer in introducing telemarketing to Jodhpur, Mr. Goyal successfully established and managed ventures in home appliance trading for several years. His professional journey further expanded through five years with Kotak Life Insurance, where he gained valuable insights into the corporate sector, financial planning, and customer relationship management. Over the years, he has also built extensive experience in the real estate industry and equity markets, developing a comprehensive understanding of investment, business operations, and wealth creation.

Driven by the belief that entrepreneurship should create value for society, Mr. Goyal's vision extends beyond personal success. He is committed to generating sustainable wealth, meaningful employment, and long-term opportunities that contribute to the prosperity of individuals, communities, and businesses alike.

This vision led to the establishment of VMighty Agro Limited, with the objective of transforming the arid regions of Rajasthan through protected cultivation. By promoting advanced polyhouse farming and modern agricultural practices, the company aims to enhance crop productivity, improve farmers' livelihoods, and contribute to the sustainable development of the agricultural sector.

As Director, Mr. Goyal is actively involved in the company's day-to-day operations, overseeing strategic planning, funding, team building, operational management, and corporate collaborations. His hands-on leadership style, combined with decades of entrepreneurial experience, enables him to guide the organization with clarity, discipline, and a long-term growth perspective.

Known for his determination, work ethic, and passion for business, Mr. Goyal embraces new ideas and opportunities with enthusiasm. His ability to identify emerging opportunities, build strong business relationships, and execute strategic initiatives continues to drive VMighty Agro Limited toward becoming a leading and profitable enterprise in the field of protected agriculture.

Director & Co-Founder
Director & Co-Founder

Mrs. Rachna Goyal

Mrs. Rachna Goyal is a Director and co-founder of VMighty Agro Limited, combining a strong foundation in science with a Master of Business Administration (MBA). Born and raised in Jodhpur, she always aspired to be a business owner and pioneer new ventures.

Her entrepreneurial journey began in 2013 when she successfully launched and managed 'MANPRIYAS', a designer boutique, for seven years. Showing exceptional creativity, determination, and work ethic, she built a thriving brand while effectively managing her responsibilities as a homemaker and mother. Following a brief break during the pandemic, she returned to business with a new focus on the manufacturing sector.

In 2022, Mrs. Goyal established VMighty Industries, a progressive MSME specializing in the manufacture of high-quality power cords for domestic appliances. Under her leadership, the company has become a trusted supplier to leading Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) across India, including brands like Prestige, Pigeon, Crompton, and Bajaj.

In 2025, she co-founded VMighty Agro Limited alongside her husband, Mr. Lokesh Goyal, stepping into the field of protected cultivation. Together, they established their flagship polyhouse project in Village Ramsar, near the Jodhpur-Jaisalmer Highway. Within this venture, Mrs. Goyal applies her extensive planning, administrative, and creative management skills to optimize farm operations, enhance productivity, and support sustainable agricultural growth. Her leadership serves as a profound motivation for her team and her family.

Director & Technical Lead
Director & Technical Lead

Manya Goyal

Manya Goyal is a dynamic young entrepreneur and Director of VMighty Agro Limited, driven by a vision to create meaningful opportunities and sustainable impact. Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering at Mahindra University (Class of 2028), she combines technical knowledge with entrepreneurial thinking to lead innovative initiatives in agriculture and business.

Inspired by her father's entrepreneurial journey, Manya began developing leadership skills early in life and has consistently sought opportunities to create value for others. She believes that true leadership lies in empowering people, fostering innovation, and building ventures that generate long-term social and economic impact.

Throughout her academic career, Manya has maintained an outstanding record of excellence, consistently ranking among the top-performing students. Her entrepreneurial journey began in 2025 when she founded and managed a jewellery-making business, gaining practical experience in business operations, customer engagement, and product development.

Beyond academics and entrepreneurship, Manya has demonstrated exceptional leadership within her university community. She serves as the Content Head of the French Club (2026) and previously contributed as a Content Member of the Entrepreneurship and Innovation Cell (2025).

Her achievements also include winning a National Quiz Competition in September 2023, reflecting her analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, and commitment to continuous learning.

As a leader, Manya enjoys taking ownership of academic, technical, and organizational projects. Her strengths lie in strategic planning, project management, content creation, entrepreneurship, and creative design. She approaches every challenge with curiosity, adaptability, and a detail-oriented mindset, enabling her to transform ideas into impactful outcomes.

At VMighty Agro Limited, Manya's long-term vision is to revolutionize agriculture through protected cultivation, improve farmers' livelihoods across Rajasthan, and create sustainable employment opportunities by promoting modern agricultural practices and innovation-driven solutions.

With interests spanning entrepreneurship, research, software development, web design, music, and leadership, Manya continues to build the skills and experience needed to become a transformative business leader committed to driving innovation and inclusive growth.
