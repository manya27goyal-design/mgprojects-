Who We Are

Pioneering Protected Agriculture in Rajasthan

Transforming Arid Lands into Flourishing Fields

Established in December 2025, V MIGHTY AGRO LIMITED was founded with a singular, bold vision: to cultivate premium green crops and bring agricultural prosperity to the desert landscapes of Phalodi, Rajasthan. The Thar Desert, historically known for its extreme climate and challenges, is now home to our modern agricultural oasis.

We combine advanced protective farming technologies with strict agronomic principles. By controlling variables inside our customized polyhouses, we bypass the harsh limitations of desert soils and windstorms, producing high-yield, nutrient-dense crops that set new benchmarks for quality, reliability, and taste.

Our Controlled-Environment Infrastructure

Our farm is an engineering marvel designed to overcome Rajasthan’s summer temperatures of up to 50°C and frequent sandstorms. Every parameter is measured, automated, and optimized.

40 Polyhouse Structures

Spanning over 5,000 square meters, our naturally ventilated dome polyhouses protect delicate crops from scorching desert heat, direct wind impact, and pest ingress, ensuring uniform crop geometry and optimal microclimates.

Automated Fertigation

Computerized systems inject precise fertilizer ratios directly into the drip lines. Water and essential nutrients are fed straight to the root zones, eliminating wastage and optimizing plant health continuously.

Climate & Humidity Automation

Sensors constantly monitor temperature and relative humidity inside the domes. Automation systems open or close vents and trigger targeted misting to mimic ideal tropical or temperate growth profiles.

Sustainable Resource Engineering

The Water Oasis: Rainwater Harvesting & RO Tech

Water is the most precious commodity in Phalodi. We have built a robust, self-reliant ecosystem to capture, purify, and recycle every drop.

01

Metal Gutter Roof Harvesters

All polyhouse domes are topped with specialized metal gutters. They collect rainwater from the roofs and channel it instantly towards ponds.

02

Lined Artificial Reservoirs

The collected rainwater flows into massive, specialized adjacent ponds lined with high-durability membranes to prevent seepage, creating an active storage reserve.

03

Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plant

Delicate flowers and exotic vegetables require precise water chemistry. Our in-house operational RO plant adjusts water TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) dynamically to meet exact plant profiles.

04

Secure Backup Tubewells

Deep backup tubewells guarantee water flow and buffer seasonal weather changes, keeping our crops lush even in the driest months.

Why Protected Farming?

The Advantages of Polyhouse Cultivation

Total Environmental Shielding

Protected from Rajasthan's fierce sandstorms, pest incursions, and intense ultraviolet radiation, keeping crops clean and undamaged.

Uncompromising Water Efficiency

Utilizes 80% less water than open field farming through targeted sub-surface drip irrigation and closed-loop pond storage.

Year-Round Production Yield

Bypass local weather seasons. We supply fresh, exotic cucumbers, zucchini, bell peppers, and roses even in mid-summer heat.

High-Consistency Geometry

Controlled nutrient and TDS levels result in crop uniformity, beautiful aesthetic appearance, and exceptional shelf life preferred by retailers.
